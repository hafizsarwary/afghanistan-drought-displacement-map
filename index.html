Key insight

2021 was the clearest crisis year in this dataset. Many northern and western provinces show drought stress, while Kabul records the largest displaced population because many people arrived there.

How to read the map

Color shows drought intensity based on vegetation conditions. Blue bubbles show the number of internally displaced people recorded in each province.

Data notes & sources

Vegetation

NDVI anomaly compares how green the land was compared with its normal 2015-2019 level.

Displacement

IDP means internally displaced person: someone forced to move within Afghanistan.
